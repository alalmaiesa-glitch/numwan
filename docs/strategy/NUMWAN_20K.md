# NUMWAN 20K — Revenue Operating System

Status: **Active**
Primary metric: **Net monthly revenue**
Target: **SAR 20,000 net / month for 3 consecutive months**
Operating constraint after stabilization: **3–4 owner hours / week maximum**

## Purpose
Numwan is being repositioned from a portfolio/display product into a self-service business asset engine.

Discovery → Preview → Product page → Checkout → Automated delivery → Upsell / repeat purchase.

Human meetings, custom proposals, and manual fulfillment are not part of the default sales path.

## Revenue model
1. Digital assets sold repeatedly.
2. Professional and commercial licensing of the same underlying asset.
3. Bundles and complementary upsells.
4. Later: subscriptions / updates for datasets and recurring intelligence.

## Initial market strategy
- Saudi Arabia is the launch advantage, not the geographic ceiling.
- Arabic and English are supported from the product layer.
- The architecture must allow later GCC, MENA, and global assets.
- Every product should be indexable by search engines and AI answer engines.

## Product rule
No invented, placeholder, or test asset may be published for sale.

A sellable product must have a real deliverable, a clear buyer, a defined outcome, a price, a license scope, a preview, and automated delivery readiness.

## Revenue gates
- Gate 1: first SAR 1,000
- Gate 2: SAR 5,000 / month
- Gate 3: SAR 10,000 / month
- Gate 4: SAR 20,000 net / month × 3 consecutive months
- Expansion target after Gate 4: SAR 50,000 / month

## Current implementation sequence
1. Store catalog and public product discovery.
2. Product detail + preview.
3. First real sellable asset.
4. Checkout/payment integration.
5. Private automated delivery.
6. Order ledger and entitlements.
7. SEO / AI-search product metadata.
8. Email lifecycle and cross-sell.
9. Affiliate/referral layer.
10. Revenue dashboard.

## Parallel innovation rule
Numwan is the cash engine. It must not absorb the main innovation track.

Only two active strategic lanes:
1. **NUMWAN 20K** — cash engine.
2. **Global technology bet** — one high-upside technical project after prior-art validation.
