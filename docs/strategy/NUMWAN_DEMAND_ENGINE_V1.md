# NUMWAN 20K — Demand Engine V1

## Objective

Build qualified demand before Product 1 checkout opens.

The demand engine must work without requiring the founder to publish manually every day or hold sales meetings.

## Public funnel

Saudi Heavy Industry Snapshot 2025
→ optional launch-interest signup
→ Product 1 page after publish
→ free sample
→ checkout
→ automated entitlement and delivery.

## Measurement

Pre-launch:
- INSIGHT_VIEW
- subscribed launch leads
- Insight → Lead conversion

Post-launch:
- PRODUCT_VIEW
- SAMPLE_DOWNLOAD
- orders created
- paid orders
- Product → Purchase conversion
- net revenue

## Lead policy

- The public snapshot is not email-gated.
- Email signup is optional and requires explicit consent.
- No IP address is stored by the lead-capture workflow.
- No purchased personal-email lists.
- Duplicate email/product signups update the existing consented record.
- Launch leads are scoped to the selected product and closely related updates.

## Target-account policy

Target accounts are organization-level research records, not scraped personal-contact lists.

Every target should have:
- a clear product-use rationale,
- a public source URL,
- a segment,
- a priority,
- and a pipeline status.

Initial priority segments:
1. Industrial market research.
2. Saudi market entry.
3. Industrial strategy and policy.
4. Sustainability / manufacturing ESG.
5. Industrial transformation and supply-chain consulting.

## Launch principle

Do not increase outreach volume until the funnel can measure:
Insight view → Lead → Product view → Sample → Order → Paid.

The goal is not reach for its own sake. The goal is qualified traffic and paid conversion.
