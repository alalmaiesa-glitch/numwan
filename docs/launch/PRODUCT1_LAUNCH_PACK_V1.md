# NUMWAN Product 1 — Launch Pack V1

## Release rule

Nothing in the LAUNCH or FOLLOW_UP phase may publish while Product 1 checkout is not READY.

Current operating states:
- PRELAUNCH assets → READY.
- LAUNCH assets → BLOCKED_PAYMENT.
- FOLLOW_UP assets → BLOCKED_PAYMENT.
- Metricool → no social network connected as of the latest check.

Connecting X and LinkedIn is therefore a launch-session input, not a blocker for preparing the campaign.

## Funnel

Organic/social/search
→ public Saudi Heavy Industry Snapshot
→ use-case landing
→ launch-interest lead
→ Product 1 page after publication
→ free sample
→ checkout
→ entitlement / secure delivery.

## Launch-day sequence

Only after payment end-to-end acceptance:
1. Move Product 1 checkout_status to READY.
2. Publish product.
3. Confirm public product page, sample and sitemap.
4. Release LinkedIn/X launch assets.
5. Send opt-in launch email to store_leads only.
6. Begin account-level outreach through public company contact channels.
7. Watch product view → sample → order → paid.

## Target-account outreach

Do not scrape or buy personal email lists.

Use:
- official company contact forms,
- published business-development or research contact channels,
- partnerships,
- public professional channels when contextually appropriate.

The target-account asset is a research-led introduction, not a bulk cold-email blast.

## Post-launch follow-up

The first follow-up focuses on data discipline:
- missing values are not invented,
- restricted directory content is excluded,
- capacity units are not incorrectly aggregated,
- confidence metadata stays visible.

This is intentionally different from a discount campaign. Trust and analytical usefulness are the differentiators.

## Decision thresholds

Before scaling distribution, diagnose the funnel:

High Snapshot views + low Leads
→ positioning or perceived value problem.

High Leads + low Product views
→ launch notification / CTA problem.

High Product views + low Sample downloads
→ product-page clarity or trust problem.

High Samples + low Checkout starts
→ price / product-fit / licensing problem.

High Checkout starts + low Paid
→ payment UX or technical problem.

Paid orders + low repeat behavior
→ catalog / cross-sell / update-value problem.

## Metricool

The Metricool brand currently has no social network connected.

When X and LinkedIn are connected:
- use existing campaign assets as the scheduling source of truth,
- do not rewrite copy during scheduling unless performance data justifies it,
- mark assets SCHEDULED when queued,
- mark assets PUBLISHED after confirmation.

The founder should not need to copy and paste routine campaign content.
