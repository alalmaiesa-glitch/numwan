## 2. Proposed Standard License

The proposed Standard License grants the purchasing legal entity a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the asset for its own internal business and analytical purposes.

Default proposal pending owner approval:
- One purchasing legal entity.
- Up to **5 internal users**.
- Internal research, market analysis, strategy, sourcing, ESG screening and planning are permitted.
- Derived calculations, internal charts and internal presentations are permitted.
- Limited derived or aggregated findings may be included in external client work provided the raw dataset, substantial extracts, or a reconstructable copy are not delivered to the third party.
- Required source attribution must be preserved when source-derived material is shown externally.

## 3. Prohibited uses

Unless a higher license expressly permits it, the purchaser may not:
- resell the raw dataset,
- redistribute the files,
- publish a substantial or reconstructable portion of the dataset,
- sublicense or share the files with another legal entity,
- make the files publicly downloadable,
- create a competing data product primarily by repackaging the dataset,
- remove required third-party source attribution,
- represent estimated or low-confidence values as audited or government-certified facts.

## 4. Source rights

Numwan's license applies to Numwan's packaging, normalization, organization, documentation and licensed access.

Underlying source rights remain subject to their own terms.

Product 1 currently includes Climate TRACE source data under CC BY 4.0. The product's Source & Rights Register identifies included sources and required attribution.

Restricted private-directory records are excluded from the commercial deliverable.

## 5. Data limitations

The purchaser acknowledges that:
- Product 1 is not a complete government census of Saudi factories.
- Coverage reflects facilities/sources represented in the documented source set.
- Some values are estimates.
- Confidence varies by record.
- Missing values are not inferred by Numwan.
- Capacity values with different units must not be aggregated as though directly comparable.
- The dataset is not legal, financial, investment, environmental-assurance or engineering advice.
